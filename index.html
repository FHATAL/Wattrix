How it works

Three inputs, then it gets out of your way

Pick your car

Choose from 48 presets or type your own battery size and consumption. Wattrix remembers it on this device for next time.

Describe the situation

Distance, starting charge, charger power, price, temperature, how you drive and what you are carrying.

Read the answer

Results update as you type. Share the link, export a CSV, print it, or save the scenario for trips you repeat.

Nothing to upload

There is no backend. Your car, your routes and your prices stay in this browser's local storage. Clearing your browser data deletes everything Wattrix knows.

The taper is modelled

Charging power drops as the battery fills. Wattrix integrates a real taper curve instead of dividing kWh by kW, which is why its 10 to 80% times look like the ones you see on the charger.

Weather counts

Cold batteries hold less and heat the cabin from the same pack. Temperature, speed, tyres and load all feed into the consumption figure the plan is built on.

Quick answers

Common questions

Do I need an account?

No, and there is nowhere to make one. Wattrix has no server-side component at all - the page you load is the whole product.

How accurate are the numbers?

Good enough to plan with, not good enough to bet your last 5% on. Consumption depends on wind, traffic, elevation and driving style, and charging depends on cell temperature and how busy the site is. Always keep the reserve you set.

Does it work without signal?

Yes. After your first visit the page is cached, so it opens and calculates with no connection - which is exactly when you tend to need it.

Which cars does it support?

Every one. There are 48 presets for common models to save typing, but any EV works if you know the usable battery size and roughly what it consumes.
